Configurable GF pair — ticket 12 synthetic PC evidence
====================================================

Public configuration: pc_bench_set_gf_pair(low,high), editor draft/commit/cancel,
then timestamped exposure through pc_bench_accept_sample or normal poll/replay,
with pc_bench_read observations. Core ostc4_exposure_set_gf_pair independently
rejects the recording lock. The serialized recording controller sets that lock
before accepting a dive-start calculation. Preferred and active pairs are
explicit atomic tuples; editor changes do not become calculation settings.

Initial pair is 30/70. Valid pairs are integer 1..100, Low<=High, equal accepted.
Cancellation/rejection preserve every committed field. A settings change uses
current tissues, ambient pressure, exposure timestamp, dive-time/max-depth state,
settings anchor and GF-low-depth input. It reruns the same contained planner
without a fake exposure segment or tissue integration. It commits only valid
results; failure restores all planner scratch. Normal replanning may grow the
persistent maximum first-stop anchor; GF edits and recording completion never
arbitrarily clear either anchor, loading or obligations.

Policy identities stay separate:
- OSTC4_POLICY and OSTC4_EXPOSURE_POLICY retain legacy fixed-30/70 evidence identity.
- OSTC4_CONFIGURABLE_GF_POLICY is ostc4-continuous-air-configurable-gf-retained-anchor-grid-time-v3.
  This corrected exposure-only variant uses exact mathematical3m stop-leg timing;
  see ../ostc4_baseline/PLANNER-TIMING-CORRECTION.txt. Historicalv2 evidence remains
  identified by its original policy and is not rewritten.
Original baseline/corrected comparison variants retain their fixed settings and
policy strings; configurable setters exist only in continuous exposure.

The independently authored oracle and vectors are frozen in tests/bench/gf-reference.
Their provenance/policy files predate production comparison and remain unchanged.
The production comparator consumes their values, never rewrites them. It tests
12 cases/114 exact-cadence events, five schedule pairs, shallow availability,
decompression and repetitive 30/70->20/60 with retained anchors/residual obligations.
Five additional shallow cases evaluate the independent frozen oracle on actual
one-second exposure cadence (455 observations). Endpoint vectors remain unused
until ticket 13; this ticket does not implement endpoint display/calculations.

Exact comparisons: NDL, each mandatory-stop slot, availability, pair/time counters.
Absolute bounds: tissue 0.0005 bar, anchor/GF-low-depth 0.0001 bar, operational ceiling
0.105 m. Only total TTS has the previously user-approved 3 s allowance. The current
comparison has no TTS differences and no failures; former fixed 30/70 exact-TTS
rejections remain in their original evidence bundles and are not rewritten.

The public-behavior test also compares subsequent anchor-sensitive observations
and checkpoint bytes after cancelled/rejected settings and hypothetical predictions
against a matched untouched control. Current tissues/timestamps/history do not
change on an accepted same-time pair change. Checkpoint v2 preserves pair+lock;
controller recovery and configuration-history limitations are in ../ostc4_baseline/CHECKPOINT.txt.

PC synthetic mathematics and native simulator rendering only. No Nordic target,
physical-watch, BLE/Android or operational dive validation is implied.

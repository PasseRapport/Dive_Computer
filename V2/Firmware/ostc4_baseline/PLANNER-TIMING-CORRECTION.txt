Exact mandatory-stop travel — corrected continuous-exposure variant v3
====================================================================

Reproduced failure
------------------
The independently frozen full schedule comparison rejects GF1/1 and1/100 after
60m/60min. A smaller public bench input reproduces TTS6688 vs6682 (+6s) at
GF1/100: fresh surface1.01325bar, advance120s to7.01325bar, advance1920s at the
same pressure. At30m/60min total TTS differs only1s but two mandatory-stop slots
differ10s, so checking total TTS alone would miss the error. Original rejected
stress evidence is retained in simulator/evidence/ticket13; corrective before/
after evidence is in simulator/evidence/numerical-correction.

Diagnosis
---------
Ranked hypotheses were planner binary32 travel/uptake accumulation, capped-output
policy mismatch, and cadence/anchor mismatch. The independent oracle author
confirmed all five minimized schedules using65-digit Decimal tissue and ceiling
math; every stop slot and TTS matched the frozen binary64 oracle. Cap, anchor and
cadence rules agree. No oracle or tolerance change was needed.

The decisive single-variable probe changed only projected travel duration.
Upstream computes ceilf((bottom-top)*(600/rate)) from rounded binary32 absolute
pressures. One nominal3m leg at45m gives bottom5.513249874bar,
top5.213249683bar, difference0.3000001907bar, duration20.000011444s and ceiling21s.
The declared policy gives exactly60*3/9=20s. Repeating the extra second changes
both ascent total and projected offgassing; later ten-second wait decisions can
therefore change. Actual tracked tissue integration was not the cause.

Narrow correction
-----------------
The generated corrected planner explicitly marks mandatory stop-to-stop legs.
Only with the existing fixed air policy (last/second/increment0.3/0.6/0.3bar and
no ascent gas changes), their mathematical height is3m and duration is computed
as ceil(60.0*3.0/ascent_rate) in binary64: exactly20s at9m/min. The original
pressure endpoints and all tissue/ceiling/stop calculations remain unchanged.
Coarse ascent probes, the noninteger0.333334bar probe, arbitrary pressure inputs,
first-stop approach and other settings retain their original duration path.
There is no epsilon snapping, rounding of real inputs, tolerance increase,
expected-value regeneration, stop bypass or accepted-GF range restriction.

The patch is applied to a hash-verified contained build copy by
corrections/correct_planner_timing.py. The pinned upstream tree and original/
corrected fixed comparison variants stay byte-identical. The correction record
is corrections/planner-timing-corrections.json. New local parameters introduce no
persistent scratch, so prediction/checkpoint state shape remains unchanged.

Validation
----------
The public regression exercises five independently confirmed schedules:
GF1/10060m30min6682s; GF1/10030m60min3472s; GF1/10060m60min21402s;
